Governance & Assurance FAQ

Clear operational answers for NHS Practice Managers, Caldicott Guardians, and Digital Transformation Leads.

Does MedOriva AI store patient consultations or NHS numbers? +
No. MedOriva AI operates on an ephemeral in-memory processing model aligned with UK GDPR data-minimisation principles. No clinical transcripts, patient names, NHS numbers, or identifiable health details are written to disk or relational databases. When an intake session terminates, active memory is purged immediately.
Does MedOriva AI replace official NHS qualified interpreters? +
No. MedOriva AI is designed to support routine, structured interactions—such as reception arrival, appointment check-in, and basic administrative queries—reducing front-desk delays. Qualified human interpreters remain essential for complex, sensitive, or high-risk clinical consultations.
How does the system distinguish positive and negative symptoms? +
Our phrase engine evaluates clinical keywords alongside multi-language negation patterns across supported dialects (e.g. detecting "illai", "nahi", "nie", "la", or "no"). If a patient says "nenji vali illai" (no chest pain), the system classifies it as a negative expression and provides a communication confirmation only, preventing false escalation alerts.
What regulatory frameworks does MedOriva AI align with in the UK? +
MedOriva AI aligns with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and NHS Digital DCB0129 clinical risk management standards, with planned pre-deployment alignment against the NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC).
