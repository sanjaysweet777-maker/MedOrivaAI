Governance & Assurance FAQ

Clear operational answers for NHS Practice Managers, Caldicott Guardians, and Digital Transformation Leads.

Does MedOriva AI store patient consultations or NHS numbers? +
No. MedOriva AI operates on an ephemeral in-memory processing model aligned with UK GDPR data-minimisation principles[cite: 1]. No clinical transcripts, patient names, NHS numbers, or identifiable health details are written to disk or relational databases[cite: 1]. When an intake session terminates, active memory is purged immediately[cite: 1].
Does MedOriva AI replace official NHS qualified interpreters? +
No. MedOriva AI is designed to support routine, structured interactions—such as reception arrival, appointment check-in, and basic administrative queries—reducing front-desk delays[cite: 1]. Qualified human interpreters remain essential for complex, sensitive, or high-risk clinical consultations[cite: 1].
How does the system distinguish positive and negative symptoms? +
Our phrase engine evaluates clinical keywords alongside multi-language negation patterns across supported dialects (e.g. detecting "illai", "nahi", "nie", "la", or "no")[cite: 1]. If a patient says "nenji vali illai" (no chest pain), the system classifies it as a negative expression and provides a communication confirmation only, preventing false escalation alerts[cite: 1].
What regulatory frameworks does MedOriva AI align with in the UK? +
MedOriva AI aligns with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and NHS Digital DCB0129 clinical risk management standards, with planned pre-deployment alignment against the NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC)[cite: 1].
