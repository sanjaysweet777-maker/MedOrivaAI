About MedOriva AI

Our mission is to eliminate linguistic disparities in emergency healthcare.

Founded in the United Kingdom, MedOriva AI was developed to address critical delays encountered by non-English speaking patients at NHS emergency reception desks and general practice appointments. In emergency medicine, 15 to 45-minute interpreter wait times directly impact clinical outcomes.

By combining instant dual-direction translation with smart clinical negation and symptom screening, MedOriva AI bridges the first critical minutes of care. Our early-access pilot program provides healthcare trusts and international endorsing bodies with an operational look at the future of compliant, safe multilingual clinical intake.

Privacy Policy (UK GDPR Aligned)

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Zero Patient Identifiable Data Storage

MedOriva AI processes patient dialogue strictly in volatile memory (RAM) during active sessions. No clinical notes, audio records, patient names, NHS numbers, or identifiable health transcripts are persisted to relational databases, object storage, or hard disks.

2. Data Minimisation Principle

In accordance with Article 5(1)(c) of the UK GDPR, data collection is limited exclusively to technical strings necessary for real-time translation during active socket lifecycles.

3. Staff Account Data

Staff credentials (email addresses and cryptographic session tokens) are maintained solely for authorized application access under the lawful basis of legitimate clinical governance.
