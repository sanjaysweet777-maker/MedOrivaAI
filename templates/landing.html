RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Designed for clear communication.
Developed with care.

Built for shared understanding

Original messages, native-script translations and understanding checks help staff and patients clarify meaning together. The workflow supports access to qualified interpreters for sensitive or complex conversations.

Demonstrate with fictional data

This prototype does not save conversation transcripts to its database. Configured full-text requests are sent to Google Cloud Translation. Provider and hosting data handling must be assessed before any real patient use.

A measured roadmap

130+ provider-supported languages is a commercial-launch target, subject to provider availability and configuration. It is not a claim of healthcare validation. Formal safety and governance assessments remain pre-deployment work.

MedOriva AI is intended to support communication. This demonstration does not provide diagnosis, clinical triage or treatment advice. NHS approval, DCB0129 conformity and DTAC assessment are not claimed.

Development and evaluation

The current prototype is being developed for practice evaluation. Independent bilingual validation and formal safety assessment are required before clinical deployment.
