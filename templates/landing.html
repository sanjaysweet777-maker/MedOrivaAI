RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT

Designed for clear communication.
Developed with care.

Built for shared understanding

Original messages, native-script translations and understanding checks help staff and patients clarify meaning together. The workflow supports access to qualified interpreters for sensitive or complex conversations.

Demonstrate with fictional data

This prototype does not save conversation transcripts to its database. Data processing occurs in volatile memory during active sessions. Provider and hosting data handling must be assessed before real patient use.

A measured roadmap

130+ provider-supported languages is a commercial-launch target, subject to provider availability and configuration. It is not a claim of healthcare validation. Formal safety and governance assessments remain pre-deployment work.

Scope Notice: MedOriva AI is intended to support communication. This demonstration does not provide diagnosis, clinical triage or treatment advice. NHS approval, DCB0129 conformity and DTAC assessment are not claimed.

Development and clinical evaluation notes

The current prototype is being developed for practice evaluation. Independent bilingual validation and formal safety assessment are required before clinical deployment in live NHS settings.
