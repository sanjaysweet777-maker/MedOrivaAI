Governance & Assurance FAQ

Direct answers for NHS Practice Managers, Caldicott Guardians, and Primary Care Digital Leads.

Does MedOriva AI store patient consultations or NHS numbers? +
No. MedOriva AI operates on an ephemeral, in-memory processing architecture aligned with UK GDPR data-minimisation principles. No clinical transcripts, audio recordings, patient names, NHS numbers, or identifiable medical details are written to disk, relational databases, or third-party training pipelines. When a session ends, active memory is purged immediately.
Does MedOriva AI replace official NHS human interpreters? +
No. MedOriva AI is designed to support routine, structured interactions—such as reception check-in, appointment confirmation, and basic administrative queries—reducing delays at point of care. Qualified human interpreters remain the appropriate standard for complex, sensitive, or high-risk clinical consultations.
How does the system handle negated symptom phrases? +
Our phrase engine couples symptom keyword scanning with multi-language negation parsing across supported dialects (e.g., detecting "illai", "nahi", "nie", "la", or "no"). If a patient reports that they do not have chest pain ("nenji vali illai"), the system classifies the statement as negative and displays a clear confirmation without triggering staff attention notifications.
What regulatory frameworks does MedOriva AI align with in the UK? +
MedOriva AI is built in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and NHS Digital DCB0129 clinical risk management standards, with planned pre-deployment assessment against the NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC).
