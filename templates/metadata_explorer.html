Future Operational Assurance & Privacy-Minimising Metadata

MedOriva has developed this illustrative front-end prototype to demonstrate how operational demand, language frequency, and workflow completion could be audited during future organisation-approved deployments without routinely retaining raw patient conversational transcripts. This demonstrator operates entirely with synthetic demonstration records and has not processed live NHS patient, staff, or practice data.

Designed to support future data-protection and clinical-safety assurance Demonstration covering 9 configured languages May support future safety monitoring, subject to formal assurance Demo organisation: Synthetic GP site
Ephemeral Interaction Payload (Not Persisted)
  • Raw typed text, native input strings, and romanised variants
  • Real-time speech audio and TTS synthesis streams
  • Patient NHS numbers, dates of birth, names, or addresses
  • Consultation dialogue content and specific symptom accounts
  • Processing state: Processed in volatile memory during active turn and cleared
Future Operational Telemetry Schema (Synthetic Concept)
  • Session reference hash and ISO UTC timestamp
  • Configured language selection (e.g. Tamil, Polish, Arabic)
  • Interaction method: Standard Typing vs. Guided Touch
  • Administrative purpose category & Understanding Check outcome
  • Latency timing band and application build version
Dataset: Synthetic Demonstration Records
Privacy status: No direct patient identifiers in this schema
